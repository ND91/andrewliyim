Andrew Y.F. Li Yim is a Tenure Track Assistant Professor Immuno-Informatics 
at the [Tytgat Institute for Liver and Intestinal Research](https://www.amsterdamumc.org/),
Department of Gastroenterology & Hepatology, Amsterdam University Medical Center and the 
University of Amsterdam.

His research sits at the interface of **computational biology and translational
medicine**, focusing on how epigenetic mechanisms — particularly DNA methylation —
associate with and drive inflammatory diseases such as inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) 
and rheumatoid arthritis. By harnessing technologies from the fields of bioinformatics and 
machine learning he seeks to move from molecular signals to and clinically actionable insights.

Andrew completed a **EU Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions–funded PhD** jointly between 
Amsterdam UMC and GlaxoSmithKline, giving him a strong bridge between academic discovery
and industry translation. He has since founded **OmiQuant**, a bioinformatics
consultancy serving life science and pharmaceutical clients.
